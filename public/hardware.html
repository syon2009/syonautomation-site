Suggested specification

Built to run the heavy ones

TIA Portal, WinCC and Studio 5000 are demanding. This spec (or better) runs all of them at once without a crawl. Bring your own laptop that meets it, or we supply one.

Minimum

Gets it running

  • Windows 11 Pro 64-bit
  • Intel Core i5 (recent gen) or Ryzen 5
  • 16 GB RAM
  • 512 GB NVMe SSD
  • 15.6" Full HD (1920×1080)
  • Gigabit Ethernet (RJ45) or included USB–Ethernet adapter
Suggested

Comfortable for all of it

  • Windows 11 Pro 64-bit
  • Intel Core i7 (12th gen or newer) / Ryzen 7
  • 32 GB RAM — several packages open at once
  • 1 TB NVMe SSD — room for every toolchain and your projects
  • 15.6" Full HD, matte; business-class build
  • RJ45 Ethernet, multiple USB-A + USB-C, SD slot
Why it matters

For this software

  • RAM is the big one — TIA Portal and Studio 5000 are memory-hungry
  • An SSD cuts project open/compile times dramatically
  • A real RJ45 port saves fighting with adapters at the panel
  • Windows Pro (not Home) for domain join and IT policies

Exact make and model are chosen at order time against the current market, so you always get a fair, up-to-date price. Have one already? We'll configure your machine instead, for less.
