What it does

From the PLC to the answer, in one window

Live tags, straight off the PLC

Connect to any OPC UA server (Kepware, FactoryTalk Linx Gateway, Ignition, Siemens S7-1500, Beckhoff), browse the address space and double-click the tags you want. They stream into a live trend at a fixed rate, so FFTs and filters are valid.

  • Anonymous or user and password, with OPC UA security
  • Record every sample to CSV
  • Send to Workspace: each tag becomes a vector

Find out why the loop is misbehaving

Pick the PV and SP and run control-loop performance: IAE, ISE and ITAE, time in band, oscillation detection, output saturation and valve reversals, with the error plotted underneath.

  • Step-response metrics with an FOPDT estimate
  • SIMC PI tuning starting point
  • Cross-correlation for lag and dead time

Vibration and ripple, in one click

The FFT spectrum and Welch PSD pull the dominant frequencies out of motor current, vibration or any fast signal, with peak amplitudes and periods listed next to the plot.

  • Butterworth low-pass and moving filters
  • Peak and MAD outlier detection
  • Every result lands in the workspace as a new vector

SPC and capability for quality

I-MR control charts with limits and run rules, histograms with Cp, Cpk, Pp and Ppk against your spec limits, and descriptive statistics, straight from live plant data.

  • Flags points beyond the limits and run-rule breaks
  • Correlation matrix across tags
  • Trend and linear regression

It speaks MATLAB

Write everyday MATLAB code: 1-based indexing, matrices, plots, tf, pid, step and bode, ode45 and fminsearch. More than 350 functions are built in, plus a full Python engine and an optional GNU Octave engine for function files.

  • Script editor, command window and workspace
  • Import CSV, Excel and .mat files
  • opc.read and opc.write in your own scripts

Questions

Frequently asked

What is DatumLab Academy, and do I need it?

A separate training app for people who use DatumLab. You describe what you see in your data and it shows the method, the clicks, a ready-to-run script and how to read the result. DatumLab works fine without it; the Academy gets new users productive faster. Modules 1 and 2 and 12 scenarios are free.

How does the Academy bundle work?

Pick a bundle and you pay for DatumLab plus the Academy at a lower price in one checkout. The thank-you page shows two keys: the LAB1- key for DatumLab and the LBA1- key for DatumLab Academy. Type the same Licensed to name in both.

What counts as a live tag?

Each tag in the Monitored tags list, plus any different tag read with opc.read in a script. Standard covers 40, Plant 200 and Unlimited any number. One OPC UA server such as Kepware can sit in front of many PLCs; DatumLab counts the tags, not the PLCs.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Your scripts still open and run, and the math works as before. Connecting to OPC UA and saving (scripts, workspaces, exports and CSV recording) are switched off until you enter a license key. Nothing is deleted.

Is it a subscription?

No. Every edition is a one-time purchase that keeps working forever. The first 12 months of updates are included; after that, the optional yearly renewal keeps you on the newest version.

Does it need the internet?

No. DatumLab runs on your PC and talks to your OPC UA servers on the plant network. The license key is checked on the PC itself.

Can I upgrade from Standard to Plant later?

Yes. E-mail sales@syonautomation.com with your Standard key and you pay the difference.

Is it a MATLAB replacement?

For everyday plant-data work, yes: signals, statistics, control and plotting. It does not run Simulink or MATLAB toolboxes, and the MATLAB-syntax engine does not run function files or classdef; use the included Octave engine for function files.
