Questions

Frequently asked

How many PCs can we put it on?

As many as you like at one facility. A plant license has no PC limit and no per-seat fee — every training PC, laptop and shop computer at that site can activate with the same key.

Do the training PCs need internet?

Each PC activates online once, then checks in at least once every 14 days to confirm the license is still active. Between check-ins the courses open with no internet needed. If a PC is offline past 14 days, one connection restores it.

Can our technicians print or save the courses?

No. The courses open only in the TechProof Reader, on a licensed PC, and every page is watermarked with your plant's name. Saving, printing, copying and right-click are turned off so the material stays inside your plant.

What if we retire or replace a PC?

Open the Reader on that PC and click Free up this PC. The activation returns to your plant and you can use it on the new machine. No call to support needed.

We have more than one plant.

Add each additional facility of the same company for $2,450, or license every site at once with the Enterprise package. Ask for a multi-plant quote.

Can we buy just one course?

Yes. Pick Single course above, choose the course you want, and you'll get a key scoped to that one course for your plant.
