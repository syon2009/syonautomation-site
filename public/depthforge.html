What it does

From a picture to a carved part

Images to 3D relief

Load a photo, logo or SVG and DepthForge builds a depth map ready for LightBurn's 3D Sliced mode, plus stepped SVG layers. On-device AI estimates depth from ordinary photos.

  • Smooth relief, flat logo, beveled coin and dome styles
  • 8-bit or 16-bit PNG at the exact DPI of your line interval
  • Live 3D preview with adjustable lighting

Settings that match your laser

Pick your source, wattage, lens and material. DepthForge calculates speed, power, frequency, pulse width, passes and cleanup layers, and writes a full settings sheet.

  • Metals: stainless, carbon and tool steel, aluminum, brass, copper, titanium, silver, gold
  • Stone: slate, river stone, black and gray granite, marble
  • Your own measured recipes replace the estimates

Laser simulator, flat or rotary

Watch the job carve pass by pass before you burn real material. Switch the rotary on to see the part turn under the beam, slice by slice.

  • Job time and depth reached, live
  • Rotary diameter, split size and wrap checks
  • Save a ready-to-go job folder with every setting

AI Assist

Clean up images for the laser on your PC: remove backgrounds, make line art and stencils, posterize into depth layers, smooth for relief. Optionally create new artwork from a description with your own image-service key.

  • Background removal in a few seconds, offline
  • Line art, stencil, layers, smooth, invert
  • Send results straight to Relief or Rotary

Material test grids

Generate a calibrated test grid of passes against speed, frequency, power or pulse width. Measure the cells and DepthForge saves your own recipe for that material and laser.

  • Each cell on its own layer, labels ready to mark
  • Ready-made grids in the sample library

Send to your laser software

One click sends the job and its settings to LightBurn, MeerK40t or EZCAD, with a checklist of anything you still set by hand. DepthForge never starts the laser.

  • LightBurn: direct import with layer settings
  • MeerK40t: loads and sets up operations
  • EZCAD: ready-to-import DXF or BMP plus a pen sheet

Questions

Frequently asked

Which lasers does DepthForge work with?

Fiber galvo lasers (Q-switched and MOPA, 20 to 100 W) driven by LightBurn, EZCAD or MeerK40t. DepthForge prepares the files and settings; your laser software runs the job.

Do I need LightBurn?

No. DepthForge also exports for EZCAD and sends jobs to MeerK40t. For LightBurn, depth maps use its 3D Sliced image mode on galvo devices.

Does it need the internet?

Only to activate your license and, if you choose, to create images from a description with your own image-service key. Everything else, including the depth AI, runs offline on your PC.

How many computers can I use it on?

A standard license covers 2 computers; the business license covers 5. You can move a license to a new computer at any time.

What happens after the first year?

DepthForge keeps working forever. Updates released in the first 12 months are included; after that an optional renewal adds another year of updates.

Are the settings guaranteed?

They are calculated starting points. Every laser and lens differs, so run a test grid; DepthForge then uses your measured numbers.
