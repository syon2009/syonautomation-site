What it does

Learn it by doing it

Live ladder, just like the controller

Every lesson has a working rung. Press the pushbuttons, toggle bits and step through the scan one rung at a time, and watch power flow the way it does on a real controller.

  • 139 live ladder simulations
  • XIC, XIO, branches, seal-ins, timers and counters
  • Step through the scan to see the order of execution

All the way to PID, JSR and fault routines

The course goes past the basics: live PID loops you can tune and watch respond, JSR subroutines, GSV and SSV, arrays and UDTs, MSG instructions, VFDs over EtherNet/IP and fault handling.

  • 21 modules, entry level to advanced
  • ISA/IEC 62443 industrial cybersecurity track
  • 44 hands-on labs, graded against real test cases

Prove it before moving on

Every module ends with a quiz. Score under 75% and the module resets, so nobody skips ahead. Instant self-checks inside each lesson catch mistakes early.

  • 210 quiz questions plus in-lesson self-checks
  • 75% pass mark on every module
  • Certificate of completion

Know exactly where you stand

The Personal Edition shows a My progress report: the module you are on, lessons and labs done, scores, time spent and anything worth a review. Team and Site editions add an admin dashboard so supervisors can see who is ready.

  • My progress report in every edition
  • Admin dashboard with technician PINs (Team and Site)
  • Add-on modules install without losing records

Questions

Frequently asked

Which PLC platforms does it cover?

The core course is Allen-Bradley Studio 5000 (ControlLogix and CompactLogix). Add-on packs cover Siemens TIA Portal (S7-1200 and S7-1500), Allen-Bradley Micro800 with Connected Components Workbench, AutomationDirect CLICK, and legacy SLC 500 and PLC-5 migration. Each platform's ladder is drawn the way its own software shows it.

Do I need Studio 5000 or a real PLC?

No. RungWise has its own simulator for every platform it teaches, so you can learn at home or on a lunch break without a controller, Studio 5000, TIA Portal or any other vendor license.

What is the difference between Personal and Team or Site?

The Personal Edition is for one learner with nothing to set up: no administrator, no password and no PIN. Team and Site add an admin dashboard where a supervisor sees each technician's progress, scores and time per module.

Can I move up from Personal later?

Yes. Installing the Team or Site edition over the Personal Edition keeps your progress.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Module 1, the Ladder Sandbox and your saved records stay open. The rest of the course needs a license key, which appears on the thank-you page right after you buy. Enter it with the license button under the RungWise name in the sidebar, and your progress carries on.

Is it a subscription?

No. Every license is a one-time purchase that keeps working, with 12 months of content updates included.
